Key rules and Chief Commissioner's rulings:
- Horse Trading: Both parties can buy during Horse Trading if they have funds left in their CPF, at the flat rate of Rs. 5 Cr per winning Crook, Corrupt, or BigNeta. Clean candidates never switch. The opposing candidate defects to your side for Rs. 5 Cr (we do not call the opponent a "seller"). The other side loses the seat, and you add it to your total: Final Strength = initial seats won + seats purchased. Higher Final Strength wins. On a tie, the party with more initial seats wins. Buying capacity is CPF divided by 5 (ignore remainders). You can choose which eligible candidates to buy.
- Factions and Kingmaker: Factions cannot pool CPF money (e.g. Rs. 4 Cr + Rs. 4 Cr is not allowed). The Kingmaker is the faction within the winning party that won the most seats (initial + bought seats combined), not merely Addas. If factions tie on seats, they bargain among themselves; if they cannot agree, it goes to a full re-election (CPF plays no role in tie-breaking).
- Breaking News: If a player suffers a Breaking News loss and their CPF is empty, they lose that seat immediately to the opposition. On a loss, deductions come from the player who played the card.
- Deficit rule: If an Attack card drains votes and no VoteBank Coins remain in that seat, the attacker gains the difference. If a Breaking News card causes a vote loss and no coins remain in that seat, the opponent gets the difference.
- Campaigns: Normal Campaign cards require Funds and Pawns from the CandyNeta sitting in that specific seat only. No cross-seat funding.
- Crooks and BigNeta: All Crooks, including BigNeta, pay Rs. 2 Cr from their own starting Fund into the CPF as a party donation. BigNeta is treated as a Crook and can be bought during Horse Trading.
- Passing: If a player has no legal card to play on their turn in Prachar, they pass (do nothing), and the other party continues.
